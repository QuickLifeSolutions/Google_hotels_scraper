## Long description (Apify actor detail)
The Google Hotels Scraper pulls structured hotel data straight from Google Travel so you can:

- Monitor price parity across Booking.com, Expedia, Agoda, and more
- Enrich travel blogs and landing pages with current hotel pricing
- Feed real-time hotel inventory into revenue management dashboards
- Automate competitor rate tracking for hospitality brands and OTAs

### What data you get
- Hotel name, URL, address, phone, rating, review count
- Nightly price range plus provider-specific offers
- Photo gallery URLs and hero thumbnail
- Stay dates, occupancy options, and currency normalization

### Why it’s different
- Optimized Playwright flow with consent bypass and resource blocking

- Outputs JSON/CSV/Parquet to your Apify dataset for easy exports
- Ready-to-use sample input covering common Google Travel queries

### Ideal users
Travel startups, hospitality revenue managers, marketing agencies, SEO teams, data scientists, and anyone who needs reliable Google Hotels data at scale.
